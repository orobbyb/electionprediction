Explore assumptions

Positive margin adjustments favor Democrats; negative adjustments favor Republicans. The same adjustment applies to every modeled partisan race's polling input. It does not change Kalshi quotes or ballot questions, and it never changes your picks. This is a shared what-if assumption, not a fitted correlated-election simulation.

No chamber-control odds are shown: this shortlist is incomplete, and joint probabilities would require a separately validated model. Candidate effects and historical back-testing are not implemented.

Race state filters the location of the election, not the residence of traders. Kalshi public data has no trader-location field.

How the prototype works · assumptions and limitations

This is an experimental comparison tool, not a calibrated election forecast. Poll support is not a win probability. We convert the leading-candidate margin using a normal error model: base standard deviation 4 percentage points for an average, 6 for a single poll, widened with fieldwork age. These assumptions are unvalidated. Poll weight decays with a 14-day half-life; the two effective weights are normalized before blending. Market probabilities are normalized only for matched two-outcome quotes summing to 98–102%.

Combined estimates require both sources unless the slider is at 0% or 100%. Missing numeric polls are not replaced with race ratings. Sources marked stale/unavailable, snapshots fetched more than 48 hours ago, and polls whose fieldwork ended more than 35 days ago are excluded from calculations but remain visible below. Displayed dates are fieldwork ends, not necessarily release dates.

Polls and markets overlap in information; blending does not treat them as independent evidence. Kalshi page snapshots do not include spread or liquidity, so no liquidity adjustment is claimed. Multi-candidate and ranked-choice contests show raw quotes, not a fabricated joint forecast. Candidate cards show the tracked field, not an exhaustive certified ballot. Individual polls are a small, separately dated selection listed by RCP, not a new poll average.

Your selections, history, exports, and print report are independent of all model settings. Old saved races remain in “All my saved calls” and can be edited in the legacy board.
